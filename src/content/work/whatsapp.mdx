<Part n="01" title="Overview">
MEGO Forex runs an internship programme. Students register, complete <abbr title="know your customer — identity verification">KYC</abbr> and sit an entry exam, train as agents, and can then refer customers — earning commission on the business they bring in.

It's recruitment, onboarding and a sales channel in one. WhatsApp is where the conversation happens; the CRM is where it's recorded, attributed and paid.
</Part>

<Part n="02" title="The message sequence">
Five approved WhatsApp marketing templates, each in English and Hindi:

1. **First contact** — the opening message.
2. **Programme info** — what the internship involves.
3. **How to join** — how to register and sit the entry exam.
4. **Deadline reminder** — registration is closing.
5. **Final hours** — last call, with an open invitation to reply to a person.

Every template carries an opt-out line, and each message has one job.
</Part>

<Part n="03" title="Making it reusable">
The first versions of the templates had the cohort ID and every date written directly into the copy. That works once. For the next cohort, every template would need rewriting and re-approval by Meta.

I reworked the copy so the cohort, deadline, results date and internship dates become **variables** filled in at send time — so each template could be approved once and reused for every cohort. I also flagged the one thing that can't be a variable: Meta doesn't allow variables in a button's label, so any date in button text has to go and the call to action has to be written date-free.
</Part>

<Part n="04" title="Consent and the human handoff">
- **Consent is recorded, not assumed.** The CRM stores WhatsApp, SMS and email consent separately, with the source, the version of the privacy notice shown and a timestamp.
- **Each lead records who handled the conversation** — the AI bot, a person, or escalated — so nobody falls through the gap between automation and the team.
- **A person is one reply away.** The final message ends: *"If something is holding you back, just reply here and someone from our team will help."*
</Part>
