<Part n="01" title="Overview">
RBP Finivis sells its white-label forex platform to a narrow set of buyers: fintechs; banks — authorised-dealer, cooperative, small finance and regional rural; and travel, education and immigration businesses whose customers need forex. I designed and ran the prospecting system that finds them, reaches the right person and records every step in the CRM.

I didn't build the tools. The work is in how they're connected — what goes in, what comes out, and what happens to a reply.
</Part>

<Part n="02" title="The challenge">
Generic lists don't work here. A target has to be the right kind of institution, often holding the right licence, and reached through a founder or senior decision-maker who already gets plenty of cold outreach.

Volume without targeting burns the sending domain and the brand. So the engine is built around precision first, then scale.
</Part>

<Part n="03" title="Design decisions">
- **Work around the tool's limits.** Evaboot's email finder needs a person's name, not just a company domain — so company-level searches run through PhantomBuster first to identify the CEO or founder, then go to Evaboot.
- **Verify before sending.** Addresses are verified before they enter a sequence, which protects deliverability for everything that follows.
- **Check the licence where it matters.** Financial institutions are checked against the Reserve Bank of India's public lists of authorised dealers and money changers before outreach.
- **A field per channel, plus one overall status.** The CRM tracks email sequence step (Email 1–5, stopped on reply or bounce), LinkedIn status (request, messages 1–3, replied) and an overall contact status through to *meeting booked* — so anyone can see exactly where a prospect is.
</Part>

<Part n="04" title="The local track: Google Maps">
Not every good partner is on LinkedIn. Travel agents, study-abroad consultancies, IELTS centres and money changers are found where their customers find them — on Google Maps.

For this track I built a search matrix pairing business types with specific localities, used to drive Google Maps extraction in PhantomBuster. The resulting lists feed sales calls, with WhatsApp follow-up through Interakt once a prospect agrees to it.
</Part>
