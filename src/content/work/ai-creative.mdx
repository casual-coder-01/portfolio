<Part n="01" title="Overview">
I produce campaign creative and short-form video for both brands with generative AI — concept, prompting, compositing, typography and final edit. Models do the rendering; the decisions about idea, composition, copy and what's allowed to ship are mine.

Financial services is a regulated category, so every piece sits inside hard rules: exact licence wording, an approved list of claims, and a consistent layout across every post.
</Part>

<Part n="02" title="RBP Finivis — “Your brand on top. Our engine underneath.”">
A white-label platform is invisible by design: the partner's brand is what customers see. The campaign turns that idea into imagery — a gloved hand delivering a sealed letter, a cufflink under a tailored sleeve, a lotus above still water.

One line of copy per piece, navy and gold, and the quiet confidence of something built to sit underneath someone else's name. The audience is B2B: founders and leaders at fintechs, banks and travel businesses.
</Part>

<Part n="03" title="MEGO Forex — “Same horizons. Closer now.”">
The consumer brand covers a range of services, from outward remittance and forex cards to insurance and education-loan facilitation through lending partners. The job was a system rather than a set of one-offs: **one service, one message and one call to action per post**, with the logo, trust line and licence wording in the same place every time.

The imagery carries the emotion — travel, study, family — while the layout does the work of making a regulated product feel simple and trustworthy.
</Part>

<Part n="05" title="The rules behind the work">
Generative tools make it easy to produce a lot of plausible, off-brand work. I keep a written set of creative rules, added to whenever feedback exposes a gap, that every piece has to pass. A selection:
</Part>

<Wide>
<Rules
  rules={[
    { title: 'One message, one call to action.', body: 'Each post does one job. Everything else is cut.' },
    { title: 'No painted buttons in static ads.', body: 'Paid social renders its own CTA button under the creative. A drawn one duplicates it and looks amateur.' },
    { title: 'Compliance copy is verbatim.', body: 'The licence line appears exactly as registered on every commercial post. Rates are marked indicative.' },
    { title: 'Claims come from an approved list.', body: 'Never “guaranteed”, “best rates” or “No. 1” — “competitive rates” instead.' },
    { title: 'Facilitated products say so.', body: 'Education loans are “through our lending partners”, never “our loans”.' },
    { title: 'Make the data visual.', body: 'A proportion drawn to scale lands harder than a percentage set in type.' },
    { title: 'No template-generated layouts.', body: 'Stock-template design tools ignore the palette, grid and tone. Layouts are built under direct control.' },
    { title: 'Numbers need sign-off.', body: 'Any creative containing a figure gets written approval before it’s scheduled.' },
  ]}
/>
</Wide>
